Mediqa is a classic serif font designed with a timeless and elegant style. Its balanced letterforms and refined serifs create a sophisticated appearance that works beautifully for clean and professional typography.

This font is perfect for logos, branding, editorial layouts, headlines, packaging, book covers, and modern typography projects. Mediqa brings a sense of clarity, elegance, and professionalism that enhances both digital and print designs.

With its classic structure and graceful details, Mediqa delivers a versatile serif style that helps create polished and visually appealing compositions.
